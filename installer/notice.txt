Patina

A virtual camera for Windows 11 that passes your webcam through to any video
app (Zoom, Teams, the Camera app, OBS) with optional background blur or
replacement.

Setup installs the app, registers the Patina camera with Windows (this is
why it needs administrator rights once), and adds a Start menu shortcut. The
app itself runs as you and lives in the system tray. The camera stays
registered even after "Quit" (it then shows a dim idle pattern), so Windows
keeps its settings, such as "Allow multiple apps to use camera at the same
time", between runs.

Included model and libraries
- MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation, landscape (Google; ONNX export from
  huggingface.co/onnx-community/mediapipe_selfie_segmentation_landscape)
  -  Apache-2.0
- ONNX Runtime (MIT) with DirectML (Microsoft), and the Microsoft Visual C++
  runtime libraries.

Optional: for the smoothest edges, download Robust Video Matting (RVM,
GPL-3.0, github.com/PeterL1n/RobustVideoMatting) into the models folder and
pick it in Settings. It is not included in this installer.
